Healthy Nutritional Plan for Dogs, Cats & Other Pets

Here is a full day nutritional diet plan for your pet to keep them healthy and active. Choose a diet suited for your pet's specific needs.

Morning

  • For Dogs: 1 cup of protein-rich kibble + ½ cup of vegetables (carrots, peas)
  • For Cats: 1 can of high-protein wet food + a small bowl of water
  • For Small Pets: A portion of oats, fruits (apple slices, bananas) and water

Afternoon

  • For Dogs: ½ cup of chicken or beef (grilled) with ½ cup of rice
  • For Cats: A small portion of tuna or salmon with a tablespoon of yogurt
  • For Small Pets: A small serving of leafy greens (lettuce, spinach)

Evening

  • For Dogs: 1 cup of wet food or ½ cup of dry kibble + a dog-safe chew treat
  • For Cats: A portion of cooked chicken or turkey with some vegetables
  • For Small Pets: A mix of seeds and a fresh fruit like a slice of cucumber

Before Bed

  • For Dogs: Small portion of healthy dog treats + a bowl of fresh water
  • For Cats: A light snack like cheese cubes or a spoon of mashed pumpkin
  • For Small Pets: A small handful of pellets or nuts
